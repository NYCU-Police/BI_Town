The painted town came from Tilemap/tilemap.png in that pack. The png itself is not in this repo.
Tiles are 16×16 with 1px spacing and no margin.
Roads, sidewalks, grass, buildings, trees, benches, lamps,
and flower beds all come from this sheet.

The pack includes people, but their clothes and hair are too similar
to tell three residents apart. Mina, Alex, and Rin stay on the
Kenney Roguelike Characters sprites in assets/characters/
(also CC0; see that folder's LICENSE.txt). Those sprites are 16×16
and are drawn at 1× so a person is the same height as a door tile.
