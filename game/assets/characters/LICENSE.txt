mina.png, alex.png, and rin.png are 16×16 crops from
Spritesheet/roguelikeChar_transparent.png (tile 16×16, 1px margin):

- mina.png: row 8, column 0 (topknot). Tunic shifted toward red.
- alex.png: row 5, column 1 (long white hair). Tunic shifted toward cyan.
- rin.png: row 7, column 1 (long brown hair). Tunic shifted toward gold.

Hair, skin, and the rest of each sprite are unchanged Kenney pixels.
The hue shift keeps the three residents distinguishable and aligned with
the event-log colors. CC0 allows this modification.
